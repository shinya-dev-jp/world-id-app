Apps

TuringVote

Social Live

Two choices. Real humans. No ranking.

TuringVote is a pure 2-choice poll app for World ID Verified Humans only. Tap A or B on short neutral questions — morning person or night owl, coffee or tea, logic or intuition — and see how other verified humans actually split. After 5 questions, a one-screen summary shows whether you lean majority or minority.

  • One vote per Verified Human per question (Sybil-resistant via World ID nullifier).
  • No points, no streaks, no rewards, no leaderboard — just how humans split.
  • No bot inflation, no duplicate accounts. Percentages reflect verified humans only.
  • Available in English and Japanese.
