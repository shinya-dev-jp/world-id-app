Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

1. Overview

This policy covers all Mini Apps published by Shinya Dev, including DailyPredict and MealPact, which operate as Mini Apps within the World App ecosystem.

2. Information We Collect

  • World ID Nullifier Hash: A unique, anonymous identifier derived from your World ID verification. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or biometric data.
  • App-specific data:
    • DailyPredict: Your prediction choices (Yes/No), streak counts, points, and accuracy statistics.
    • MealPact: Meal photos (processed server-side by Gemini AI, not retained), estimated calories/macros, challenge participation data, and on-chain WLD deposit events.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the core functionality of each Mini App.
  • To prevent duplicate participation (one verified human per action).
  • To display leaderboards, achievements, and aggregate statistics.
  • To improve app experience through anonymous usage analytics.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on Supabase servers located in the Asia-Pacific region. We use Row Level Security (RLS) to protect your data at the database level.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Aggregate, anonymized statistics may be displayed publicly (e.g., "62% of humans predicted Yes").

Meal photos are processed by Google Gemini API for calorie estimation. Images are not retained beyond the API call.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: View your activity and statistics within each app.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • Portability: Request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Mini Apps require World ID verification, which is restricted to users who have completed identity verification via the Orb or equivalent. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at shinya.yuda.b@gmail.com.
